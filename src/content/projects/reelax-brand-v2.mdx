Reelax Brand v2 is where I spend my days now. It is the rebuilt brand app, and I own large parts of it.

**Reports.** I built the Competitor Analysis and Brand Insight reports end to end on the frontend: a create form with a 1-year lookback and an Always On period picker, generation-status polling, credit-confirmed refreshes, market-positioning quadrant charts, influencer-overlap Venn diagrams, PDF download and CSV exports that fetch every page.

**Collections.** Brands can create, share, bulk-select and delete creator lists, and add influencers in batches with live sync feedback.

**Auth and roles.** Email OTP login, sign-up, invite links and contact us. Roles from the v2 permissions API ride on the session, gate routes and navigation, hide actions a member cannot take, and are enforced again in server actions.

**Earlier work.** Gmail OAuth and a 161-line WhatsApp Embedded Signup module, with DTO mapping and tests, and HTTPS local development for Meta's SDK.

Next time I would agree the report-status contract with the backend before building the UI. Polling, gating and refresh states were easier to build once the states were named.
