I wanted to know how brands could keep large creator lists usable when every team adds different custom fields. The same question applied to campaign analytics: lots of numbers, not much signal.

I centralised table mapping, filters and export in one helper module (~4,000 lines) on React 18, Redux and ag-Grid, with typed custom fields and batched Excel export. Analytics screens became sections with tooltips and metric cards; credit-gated actions ask for confirmation before the API call.

I would carve the helper into filter, export and column-mapping files once a second screen needed the same behaviour, instead of waiting until the module was already large.
